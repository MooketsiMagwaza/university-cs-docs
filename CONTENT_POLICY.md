# Educational Content and Resource Policy

This policy protects students, contributors, authors, and the project when educational material is added to University CS Docs.

## Original content

Original explanations, code, diagrams, questions, and other contributions are accepted under the repository's MIT License. Contributors must have the right to submit their work.

## Third-party material

The repository may link to or, where redistribution is authorized, host course outlines, lecture material, papers, and other references. Their inclusion does not imply that the MIT License applies to them. Copyright and license notices supplied with a resource must be preserved.

Every newly hosted third-party resource must identify:

- title and author or rights holder;
- original source or provenance;
- license, public-domain status, or permission basis;
- date obtained;
- contributor who verified the above.

When redistribution rights are unclear, link to the authorized source instead of copying the file.

## Prohibited submissions

Do not submit:

- paid, pirated, or access-controlled books and articles;
- files copied from a restricted learning-management system without permission;
- leaked, confidential, or unreleased assessments and answer keys;
- student submissions, grades, identifiers, or other personal data;
- malicious files or resources that cannot be safely previewed;
- fabricated citations, sources, permissions, or course claims.

## Academic integrity

This project supports learning, not misconduct. Practice and past assessment material should be clearly identified. Do not represent generated questions as official University of Botswana examinations. Contributors must not add current confidential assessment answers or encourage submitting repository material as a student's own assessed work.

## Accuracy and AI assistance

Content must be checked against authoritative sources and independently verified. Substantial AI assistance must be disclosed in the pull request. AI output is not evidence: contributors remain accountable for correctness, originality, citations, and licensing.

## Takedowns and corrections

Report a licensing, privacy, attribution, or academic-integrity concern through a Content issue unless public disclosure would expose sensitive information. For sensitive concerns, contact the maintainer privately through the method listed on [Mooketsi Magwaza's profile](https://github.com/MooketsiMagwaza).

Maintainers may temporarily remove disputed content while investigating. Valid notices will be handled promptly, and history may be rewritten when required to remove sensitive or unlawfully distributed material.
