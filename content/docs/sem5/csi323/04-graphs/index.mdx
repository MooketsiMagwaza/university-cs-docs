A graph models things and the connections between them, such as roads between towns or links between pages. This topic covers the vocabulary of graphs and the two standard ways to store one in memory, adjacency lists and adjacency matrices, and what each costs.

<Callout type="note" title="Notes still to come">This topic has a visual study map, a review and practice questions. Written lesson notes are still being prepared.</Callout>

<LearningObjectives items={[
  'Use graph terminology correctly, and tell the main types of graph apart',
  'Store a graph as an adjacency list and as an adjacency matrix',
  'Compare the two representations by memory use and by the cost of common operations',
]} />
