title: "Regression Analysis"
description: "Fitting a line to data by least squares, and judging the fit with the ANOVA identity, correlation and the coefficient of determination."
---

Regression fits a straight line to data so that one variable can be predicted from another. This topic derives the least squares line by minimising the sum of squared errors, then asks how good the fit is: it splits the variation into explained and unexplained parts, defines the correlation and the coefficient of determination, and names the traps in interpreting them.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  'Derive the ordinary least squares estimators by minimising the sum of squared errors',
  'Fit a regression line to data, and interpret its slope and intercept',
  'Split the total variation as SST = SSR + SSE, and compute R squared',
  'Compute the Pearson correlation, and avoid the common mistakes in interpreting it',
]} />
