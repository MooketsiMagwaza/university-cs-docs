---
title: "Conditional Probability & Independence"
description: "How new information changes a probability, how to split and reverse conditional probabilities, and when two events do not affect each other."
---

Probabilities change when you learn something. This topic defines the probability of an event given another, chains conditional probabilities across stages, splits a probability over cases with the law of total probability, reverses a conditional probability with Bayes' theorem, and states exactly what it means for two events to be independent.

  'Calculate a conditional probability, and use the multiplication rule for events that happen in stages',
  'Apply the law of total probability and Bayes theorem to reverse a conditional probability',
  'Test whether two events are independent, and explain why independence is not the same as being mutually exclusive',
]} />
