# Chapter 3: Conditional Probability & Independence

This chapter explores how probabilities are updated when new information is acquired. We will establish the formal definition of conditional probability, derive the Law of Total Probability and Bayes' Theorem, and rigorously define statistical independence, distinguishing it clearly from mutual exclusivity.
