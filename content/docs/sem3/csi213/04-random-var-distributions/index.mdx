# Chapter 4: Random Variables & Distribution Functions

This chapter establishes the rigorous mathematical bridge between abstract sample spaces and the real number line. We will formally define random variables as measurable functions, explore the foundational properties of Probability Mass Functions (PMFs), Probability Density Functions (PDFs), and Cumulative Distribution Functions (CDFs), and derive the computational proofs for Expectation and Variance.

*(Note: Specific canonical models like the Binomial, Poisson, and Geometric distributions are covered in Chapter 5, building directly upon the machinery established here.)*
