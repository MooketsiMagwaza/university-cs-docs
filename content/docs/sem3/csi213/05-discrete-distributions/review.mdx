## Question bank

The questions are grouped by distribution. Choose the groups you want and how many questions to answer; every question shows its worked steps once you have answered.

The geometric and negative binomial questions count trials up to and including the success, so the support starts at 1 and the geometric mean is $\frac1p$. Lesson [5.3](/docs/sem3/csi213/05-discrete-distributions/notes/geometric-negative-binomial) counts failures before the success instead, so its formulas differ. Do not mix the two conventions in one answer.
