## Model-selection map

| If the question fixes... | And counts... | Model |
|---|---|---|
| One yes/no trial | Success as 0 or 1 | Bernoulli |
| $n$ independent trials | Number of successes | Binomial |
| First success | Trials or failures until it occurs | Geometric |
| $r$-th success | Trials or failures until it occurs | Negative binomial |
| An interval with a stable event rate | Number of events | Poisson |
| A sample size without replacement | Number of successes | Hypergeometric |

## Core formulas

| Distribution | Support and PMF | Mean | Variance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bernoulli$(p)$ | $x\in\{0,1\}$; $P(X=x)=p^x(1-p)^{1-x}$ | $p$ | $p(1-p)$ |
| Binomial$(n,p)$ | $x=0,\ldots,n$; $P(X=x)=\binom nxp^x(1-p)^{n-x}$ | $np$ | $np(1-p)$ |
| Geometric$(p)$, total trials | $k=1,2,\ldots$; $P(G=k)=(1-p)^{k-1}p$ | $1/p$ | $(1-p)/p^2$ |
| Negative binomial$(r,p)$, total trials | $x=r,r+1,\ldots$; $P(X=x)=\binom{x-1}{r-1}p^r(1-p)^{x-r}$ | $r/p$ | $r(1-p)/p^2$ |
| Poisson$(\mu)$ | $x=0,1,\ldots$; $P(X=x)=e^{-\mu}\mu^x/x!$ | $\mu$ | $\mu$ |
| Hypergeometric$(N,K,n)$ | $P(X=x)=\dfrac{\binom Kx\binom{N-K}{n-x}}{\binom Nn}$ | $nK/N$ | $n(K/N)(1-K/N)\dfrac{N-n}{N-1}$ |

## Negative binomial: the two conventions

| Quantity | Total trials $X$ | Failures $Y$ |
|---|---|---|
| Definition | Trial number of the $r$-th success | Failures before the $r$-th success |
| Support | $r,r+1,\ldots$ | $0,1,2,\ldots$ |
| Relationship | $X=Y+r$ | $Y=X-r$ |
| PMF | $\binom{x-1}{r-1}p^r(1-p)^{x-r}$ | $\binom{y+r-1}{r-1}p^r(1-p)^y$ |
| Mean | $r/p$ | $r(1-p)/p$ |
| Variance | $r(1-p)/p^2$ | $r(1-p)/p^2$ |

<Callout type="warn" title="Write the convention before the formula">
If the question says “trial number,” use $X$. If it says “number of failures,” use $Y$. Never combine the support from one convention with the PMF or mean from the other.
</Callout>

## Cumulative shortcuts

For $X\sim\operatorname{NB}(r,p)$ counting total trials and $B_k\sim\operatorname{Bin}(k,p)$:

$$
P(X>k)=P(B_k\le r-1),\qquad
P(X\le k)=P(B_k\ge r).
$$

For $G\sim\operatorname{Geo}(p)$ counting total trials:

$$
P(G>k)=(1-p)^k,\qquad
P(G\le k)=1-(1-p)^k.
$$

For $X\sim\operatorname{Bin}(n,p)$:

$$P(X\ge k)=1-P(X\le k-1).$$

For a Poisson rate $\lambda$ over a window of length $t$ in matching units:

$$
\mu=\lambda t,\qquad P(X=x)=\frac{e^{-\mu}\mu^x}{x!}.
$$

## Four-line exam template

1. **Define:** “Let $X$ be ...” and state exactly what it counts.
2. **Model:** write the distribution, parameters and support.
3. **Translate:** convert words such as “at most,” “by,” or “more than” into an event.
4. **Calculate:** show the formula, substitute, evaluate, and interpret the result.

## Language translator

| Phrase | Mathematical event |
|---|---|
| Exactly $k$ | $X=k$ |
| At most $k$ / no more than $k$ | $X\le k$ |
| Fewer than $k$ | $X\le k-1$ for integer-valued $X$ |
| At least $k$ | $X\ge k$ |
| More than $k$ | $X\ge k+1$ or $X>k$ |
| By trial $k$ | $X\le k$ |
| On trial $k$ | $X=k$ |
