## Recommended revision order

1. Read [Geometric and Negative Binomial](/docs/sem3/csi213/05-discrete-distributions/notes/geometric-negative-binomial) and reproduce all three worked examples without looking.
2. Use the [cheat sheet](/docs/sem3/csi213/05-discrete-distributions/cheat-sheet) until you can choose a model from wording alone.
3. Complete the topic assessment at the bottom of the note, then the mixed [review](/docs/sem3/csi213/05-discrete-distributions/review).
4. Finish with the [worked practice questions](/docs/sem3/csi213/05-discrete-distributions/questions), writing full solutions before opening the answers.
