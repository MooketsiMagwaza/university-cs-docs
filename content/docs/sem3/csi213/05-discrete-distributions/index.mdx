---
title: "Discrete Probability Distributions"
description: "The standard models for counting outcomes: Bernoulli, binomial, Poisson, geometric, negative binomial and hypergeometric, with their means and variances."
---

A few experiments come up again and again, so each has a named distribution with a formula. This topic builds the discrete ones from the experiment they describe: successes in independent trials, rare events in an interval, failures before a success, and draws without replacement. The skill is choosing the right model from a description, then using its probabilities, mean and variance.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  'Choose the discrete model that fits an experiment from its description',
  'Calculate probabilities, the mean and the variance for the binomial and Poisson distributions',
  'Work with the geometric and negative binomial distributions, and check which counting convention a question uses',
  'Explain what changes when sampling is without replacement, and use the hypergeometric distribution',
]} />

<Prerequisites items={[
  'Topic 4: random variables, the PMF, expected value and variance',
  'Topic 1: combinations, which appear in the binomial and hypergeometric formulas',
]} />
