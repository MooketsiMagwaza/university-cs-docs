# Chapter 5: Discrete Probability Distributions

This chapter applies the foundational machinery of random variables, PMFs, Expectation, and Variance (established in Chapter 4) to the canonical models of discrete probability. We will rigorously define the Bernoulli, Binomial, Poisson, Geometric, Negative Binomial, and Hypergeometric distributions, complete with combinatorial intuition and analytical proofs of their properties.
