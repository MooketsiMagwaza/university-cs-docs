## 1. General continuous random variable

### Valid PDF conditions

$$f(x)\ge0,\qquad \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f(x)\,dx=1.$$

### Probabilities

$$P(a<X<b)=\int_a^b f(x)\,dx,$$

$$P(X=c)=0,$$

$$P(a<X<b)=P(a\le X\le b).$$

For a narrow interval, $P(x<X<x+\Delta x)\approx f(x)\Delta x$.

### CDF

$$F(x)=P(X\le x)=\int_{-\infty}^x f(t)\,dt,$$

$$f(x)=F'(x),$$

$$P(a<X\le b)=F(b)-F(a),\qquad P(X>b)=1-F(b).$$

### Expectation and variance

$$E[X]=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}xf(x)\,dx,$$

$$E[g(X)]=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}g(x)f(x)\,dx,$$

$$E[X^2]=\int_{-\infty}^{\infty}x^2f(x)\,dx,$$

$$\operatorname{Var}(X)=E[X^2]-E[X]^2,$$

$$\operatorname{SD}(X)=\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(X)}.$$

If $Y=aX+b$,

$$E[Y]=aE[X]+b,$$

$$\operatorname{Var}(Y)=a^2\operatorname{Var}(X),\qquad \operatorname{SD}(Y)=|a|\operatorname{SD}(X).$$

## 2. Continuous uniform distribution

For $X\sim U(a,b)$:

$$
f(x)=
\begin{cases}
\dfrac1{b-a},&a\le x\le b,\\
0,&\text{otherwise},
\end{cases}
$$

$$
F(x)=
\begin{cases}
0,&x\lt a,\\
\dfrac{x-a}{b-a},&a\le x\le b,\\
1,&x\gt b,
\end{cases}
$$

$$P(c<X<d)=\frac{d-c}{b-a}\quad(a\le c<d\le b),$$

$$E[X]=\frac{a+b}{2},\qquad \operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac{(b-a)^2}{12},\qquad \operatorname{SD}(X)=\frac{b-a}{\sqrt{12}}.$$

## 3. Exponential distribution

For $X\sim\operatorname{Exp}(\lambda)$, $\lambda\gt0$:

$$f(x)=\lambda e^{-\lambda x},\quad x\ge0.$$

$$F(x)=1-e^{-\lambda x},$$

$$P(X\gt x)=e^{-\lambda x},$$

$$P(a\lt X\lt b)=e^{-\lambda a}-e^{-\lambda b},$$

$$E[X]=\frac1\lambda,\qquad E[X^2]=\frac2{\lambda^2},$$

$$\operatorname{Var}(X)=\frac1{\lambda^2},\qquad \operatorname{SD}(X)=\frac1\lambda.$$

### Solve backwards

$$P(X\gt x)=\alpha\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x=-\frac{\ln\alpha}{\lambda},$$

$$P(X\gt t)=\alpha\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \lambda=-\frac{\ln\alpha}{t}.$$

### Memoryless property

$$P(X\gt s+t\mid X\gt s)=P(X\gt t).$$

## 4. Normal distribution

For $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$:

$$f(x)=\frac1{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-\frac12\left(\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\right)^2},\qquad -\infty\lt x\lt\infty.$$

$$E[X]=\mu,\qquad \operatorname{Var}(X)=\sigma^2.$$

### Standardise

$$Z=\frac{X-\mu}{\sigma}\sim N(0,1),$$

$$x=\mu+z\sigma.$$

Let $\Phi(z)=P(Z\le z)$:

$$P(X\le x)=\Phi\left(\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\right),$$

$$P(X\gt x)=1-\Phi\left(\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\right),$$

$$P(a\lt X\lt b)=\Phi\left(\frac{b-\mu}{\sigma}\right)-\Phi\left(\frac{a-\mu}{\sigma}\right).$$

### Symmetry

$$\Phi(-z)=1-\Phi(z),$$

$$P(Z\gt z)=P(Z\lt-z),$$

$$P(-z\lt Z\lt z)=2\Phi(z)-1.$$

### Empirical rule

| Interval | Approximate probability |
|---|---|
| $\mu\pm\sigma$ | $68\%$ |
| $\mu\pm2\sigma$ | $95\%$ |
| $\mu\pm3\sigma$ | $99.7\%$ |

### Linear normal transformation

If $X\sim N(\mu,\sigma^2)$ and $Y=aX+b$, then

$$Y\sim N(a\mu+b,a^2\sigma^2).$$

## 5. Exam-language translator

| Wording | Operation |
|---|---|
| At most $x$ / before $x$ | Left area: $F(x)$ |
| More than $x$ / survives beyond $x$ | Right area: $1-F(x)$ |
| Between $a$ and $b$ | $F(b)-F(a)$ |
| Top $q\%$ | Convert to left area $1-q$, find $z$, then use $x=\mu+z\sigma$ |
| Bottom $q\%$ | Find $z$ for left area $q$, then use $x=\mu+z\sigma$ |
| Central $c\%$ | Leave $(1-c)/2$ in each tail |
