# Chapter 6: Continuous Distributions & Reliability

This chapter explores continuous probability models, focusing on the Uniform, Exponential, and Normal distributions. We will master the art of standardization and Z-table applications, and then apply these probabilistic tools to reliability engineering, analyzing system configurations and proving the foundational link between constant hazard rates and the Exponential distribution.
