title: "Continuous Random Variables & Distributions"
description: "PDFs, CDFs, integration, expectation and variance, followed by uniform, exponential and normal models and standardisation."
---

When a quantity can take any real value in an interval, probability is represented by **area**, not by the height of a single point. This chapter starts with the general rules for a continuous probability density function and cumulative distribution function, builds expectation and variance from integrals, and then applies those ideas to the uniform, exponential and normal distributions.
