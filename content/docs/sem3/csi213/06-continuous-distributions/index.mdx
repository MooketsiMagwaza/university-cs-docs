---
title: "Continuous Distributions & Reliability"
description: "The uniform, exponential and normal distributions, standardising with the Z-table, and the reliability of systems built from components."
---

When a quantity can take any value in an interval, probabilities become areas under a curve. This topic covers the continuous uniform and exponential distributions, the normal distribution with the Z-table for finding its probabilities, and the reliability of a system built from components, including the hazard rate that describes how likely a component is to fail.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  'Calculate probabilities, the mean and the variance for the continuous uniform and exponential distributions',
  'Standardise a normal variable, and use the Z-table forwards and backwards',
  'Calculate the reliability of a system from the reliabilities of its components',
  'Interpret the hazard rate of a component',
]} />
