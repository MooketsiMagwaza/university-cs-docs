## Recommended revision route

1. Start with [Continuous Random Variables, PDFs and CDFs](/docs/sem3/csi213/06-continuous-distributions/notes/continuous-pdfs). Do not move on until you can normalise a PDF and calculate $E[X]$ and $\operatorname{Var}(X)$.
2. Work through [Uniform and Exponential](/docs/sem3/csi213/06-continuous-distributions/notes/continuous-exponential), paying special attention to left tail versus survival and to rate units.
3. Complete [Normal Distribution and Standardisation](/docs/sem3/csi213/06-continuous-distributions/notes/normal-standardization), including both forward and inverse examples.
4. Use [Reliability and Hazard](/docs/sem3/csi213/06-continuous-distributions/notes/reliability-hazard) if it is within the assessment boundary.
5. Recall formulas with the [flashcard review](/docs/sem3/csi213/06-continuous-distributions/review), then complete the [written practice questions](/docs/sem3/csi213/06-continuous-distributions/questions) under timed conditions.
