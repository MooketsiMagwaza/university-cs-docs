---
title: "Introduction to Probability"
description: "Sample spaces, events, the axioms of probability and the theorems that follow, and probabilities found by counting equally likely outcomes."
---

A probability is a number attached to an event, and the axioms say which numbers are allowed. This topic starts from the experiment and its sample space, states the three axioms, proves the standard theorems from them, and then uses the counting rules from topic 1 to find probabilities when every outcome is equally likely.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  'Describe a random experiment by its sample space and events, and decide when events are mutually exclusive',
  'State the three axioms of probability and use them to prove the standard theorems',
  'Calculate probabilities in a finite sample space with equally likely outcomes by counting',
]} />

<Prerequisites items={[
  'Topic 1: set notation and operations, because events are sets',
  'Topic 1: permutations and combinations, which are used to count outcomes',
]} />
