Starting from scratch is not the only way into business. This topic compares two alternative routes, operating a franchise and buying an existing business, and follows the lecturer's Chapter 4 student notes, the class slides and the Two Men and a Truck case.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Define franchising and explain franchise terminology.",
  "Explain the advantages and disadvantages of franchising and the main franchising arrangements.",
  "Describe the process for evaluating a franchise opportunity, including the costs.",
  "Explain four reasons for buying an existing business.",
  "Describe how to evaluate, value and close the purchase of an existing business.",
]} />
