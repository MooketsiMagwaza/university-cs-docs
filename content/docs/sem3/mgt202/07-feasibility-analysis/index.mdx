title: "Feasibility Analysis"
description: "Topic 7 learning path: testing whether a business idea deserves further development through product or service, industry and target market, organizational and financial feasibility, with break-even and cash examples and a feasibility template."
---

An idea becomes a business opportunity only when research shows that customers value it, the market can support it, the team can deliver it and the finances are credible. This topic follows the lecturer's Topic 7 student notes and works the Campus Laundry Collect case through all four areas.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Explain what feasibility analysis is and why entrepreneurs do it before major commitments.",
  "Describe product or service feasibility (desirability and demand) and industry and target market feasibility (industry and target market attractiveness).",
  "Explain organizational feasibility: management prowess and resource sufficiency.",
  "Evaluate financial feasibility: start-up cash needed, the performance of similar businesses and overall financial attractiveness, including contribution, break-even and cash flow.",
  "Use a feasibility analysis template and say when to use it.",
]} />
