Family firms combine two systems that run on different rules: a family built on loyalty and a business built on performance. This topic follows the lecturer's Chapter 5 student notes and class presentation, checked against the textbook chapter and the Iaccarino & Son case.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Define family and family business, and classify the three structural stages of a family business.",
  "Explain the forces that keep a family business moving forward: the founder's imprint, socio-emotional wealth (the FIBER model) and commitment profiles.",
  "Describe the roles and relationships in a family business: co-preneurs, parents and children, siblings and in-laws.",
  "Identify the governance practices that help a family business function: retreats, councils, constitutions and fair treatment of non-family staff.",
  "Describe the process of managerial succession and ownership transfer.",
]} />
