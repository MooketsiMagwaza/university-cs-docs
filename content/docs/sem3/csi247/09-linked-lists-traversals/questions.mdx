## Question 1: draw references [12]

Draw an empty singly linked list, then show each state after adding 12, 27, and 41 at the front. Label head, data, next, and null.

## Question 2: trace traversal [10]

Trace <code>contains(27)</code> and <code>contains(99)</code> on 12 → 27 → 41. Show <code>current</code>, the evaluated equality, and the next reference after every iteration.

## Question 3: insert and delete [16]

Starting with 10 → 20 → 30, insert 25 after 20, then remove 10, 25, and 30. Redraw the complete list after every reference assignment and identify head/tail changes.

## Question 4: implementation [14]

Implement generic <code>addFirst</code>, <code>addLast</code>, and <code>removeFirst</code> with empty-list handling and a size field.

## Question 5: properties [8]

Compare ArrayList and a singly linked list for indexed access, insertion at the front, memory overhead, cache locality, and resizing. Neither earns credit as “always faster.”
