Selection sort scans the whole unsorted suffix, remembers the minimum index, and places that value into the next final position with at most one swap per pass.

The [complete visual chapter](/files/sem3/csi247/study-guides/sorting-and-searching/selection-sort.html) includes the whole-run map, guided passes, concise trace, annotated Java, properties, practice and explained quiz answers.
