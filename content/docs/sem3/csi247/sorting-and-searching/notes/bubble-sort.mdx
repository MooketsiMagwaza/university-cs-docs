Bubble sort compares neighbouring values, swaps an inverted pair, and fixes the largest remaining value at the right edge after a complete ascending pass.

The [complete visual chapter](/files/sem3/csi247/study-guides/sorting-and-searching/bubble-sort.html) includes the whole-run map, guided passes, concise trace, annotated Java, properties, practice and explained quiz answers.
