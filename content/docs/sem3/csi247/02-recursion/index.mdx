---
title: "Recursion"
description: "Understand a recursive method as stacked unfinished calls, then trace, design, and compare it with iteration."
---

A recursive method is a sequence of separate calls, each with its own parameters and local variables, waiting on the call stack. This topic shows the base case, the recursive case, and the combining step of a recursive method, then traces the calls going down and the returns coming back up. It ends by designing a recursive method and comparing it with an iterative one.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Identify the base case, the recursive case, and the combining step of a recursive method",
  "Trace a recursive call down the call stack and back up through its returns",
  "Trace and write a recursive array search",
  "Compare a recursive method with an iterative one in time and extra space",
  "Design a recursive method, and diagnose a base case that is never reached",
]} />
