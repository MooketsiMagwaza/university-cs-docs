Six questions, grouped by what they test, each with its marks. Work a question on paper with a trace table before opening the solution. The lessons that teach each form are [Recursion by Example](/docs/sem3/csi247/02-recursion/notes/recursion-by-example) and [Recursion Exam Practice](/docs/sem3/csi247/02-recursion/notes/recursion-exam-practice), which has ten more built from Lecture 6 and Lab 3.

**Marking guide.** A trace receives full credit only when the calls going down and the returns coming up are shown separately. A base case alone is insufficient: the recursive case must make measurable progress towards it, and the base case must cover every input that can reach the method.
