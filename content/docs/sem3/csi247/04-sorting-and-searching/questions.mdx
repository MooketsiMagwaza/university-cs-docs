Six questions, grouped by what they test, each with its marks. Work a question on paper before opening the solution. The lessons that teach each form are [Searching by Example](/docs/sem3/csi247/04-sorting-and-searching/notes/searching-by-example), [Bubble, Insertion and Selection Sort by Example](/docs/sem3/csi247/04-sorting-and-searching/notes/simple-sorts-by-example), [Merge Sort by Example](/docs/sem3/csi247/04-sorting-and-searching/notes/merge-sort-by-example) and [Searching and Sorting Exam Practice](/docs/sem3/csi247/04-sorting-and-searching/notes/searching-and-sorting-exam-practice), which has eight more.

**Marking guide.** A full-credit answer writes the whole array after every pass, gives `low`, `high` and `mid` for every pass of a binary search, counts comparisons from the first element, states the result as an index or -1, and gives the time complexity with its case (best, average or worst).
