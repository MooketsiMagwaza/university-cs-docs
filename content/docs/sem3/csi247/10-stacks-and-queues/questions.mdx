Six questions, grouped by what they test, each with its marks. Work a question on paper before opening the solution. The lessons that teach each form are [Stack Implementations by Example](/docs/sem3/csi247/10-stacks-and-queues/notes/stack-implementations-by-example), [Queue Implementations by Example](/docs/sem3/csi247/10-stacks-and-queues/notes/queue-implementations-by-example) and [Stacks and Queues Exam Practice](/docs/sem3/csi247/10-stacks-and-queues/notes/stacks-and-queues-exam-practice), which has eight more.

**Marking guide.** A trace receives full credit only when the structure is shown after every operation with the top, front and tail marked. A diagram needs the object that holds `top` (or `front` and `tail`), the array or the nodes with their addresses, every reference as an arrow, and unreachable nodes marked as garbage. A method answer must handle the empty structure and, for arrays, the full one.
