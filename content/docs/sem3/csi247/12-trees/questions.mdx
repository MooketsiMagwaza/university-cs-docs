Insert <code>45, 28, 72, 18, 34, 60, 90, 31</code> into an empty BST. [12]

1. Label root, leaves, parent/children of 28, height, and subtree rooted at 72. [8]
2. Give preorder, inorder, postorder, and level-order traversals. [12]
3. Trace search for 31 and 50 with every evaluated comparison. [10]
4. Delete 18, then 34, then 45; redraw after every deletion. [18]
5. Explain why insertion order changes height and therefore performance. [6]
