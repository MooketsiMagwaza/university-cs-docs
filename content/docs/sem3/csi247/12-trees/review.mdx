- BST invariant: every left key is smaller; every right key is larger under the chosen duplicate policy.
- Search/insert/delete cost O(h), not automatically O(log N).
- inorder traversal of a valid BST gives sorted order.
- recursive traversal always needs a null base case.
- deletion has leaf, one-child, and two-child cases.
