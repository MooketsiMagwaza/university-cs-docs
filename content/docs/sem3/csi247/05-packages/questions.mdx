Five questions, grouped by what they test, each with its marks. Work a question on paper before opening the solution. The lessons that teach each form are [Packages by Example](/docs/sem3/csi247/05-packages/notes/packages-by-example) and [Packages Exam Practice](/docs/sem3/csi247/05-packages/notes/packages-exam-practice), which has seven more.

**Marking guide.** A full-credit answer names the failing step, quotes the compiler or launcher message, and gives the exact repair: a folder named like the package, `public` on the class, `-classpath classes` on the command. For a layout, show `src`, the package folder and `classes`, and the folder the commands are typed from.
