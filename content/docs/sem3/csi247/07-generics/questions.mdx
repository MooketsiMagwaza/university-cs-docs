Six questions, grouped by what they test, each with its marks. Work a question on paper before opening the solution. The lessons that teach each form are [Generics by Example](/docs/sem3/csi247/07-generics/notes/generics-by-example) and [Generics Exam Practice](/docs/sem3/csi247/07-generics/notes/generics-exam-practice), which has eight more.

**Marking guide.** A full-credit answer connects every use of `T` to the type argument, gives the exact reason a statement does not compile (a primitive, the wrong type, mismatched types on the two sides of `=`, a type outside the bound), and states the safe way to write it. Say "found at compile time" when the point is type safety.
