## Question 1: choose the abstraction [12]

Choose List, Set, Queue, or Map for a playlist, unique registered module codes, printer jobs, and marks by student number. For each, state the required behaviour before naming an implementation.

## Question 2: read generic declarations [10]

For <code>Map&lt;String, List&lt;Book&gt;&gt;</code>, identify key and value types, then write legal <code>put</code> and <code>get</code> expressions. Explain what compile-time errors prevent.

## Question 3: trace mutations [12]

Trace <code>add</code>, indexed insertion, <code>set</code>, value removal, and index removal on an ArrayList. Draw the list after every statement and distinguish the two overloaded <code>remove</code> methods for <code>List&lt;Integer&gt;</code>.

## Question 4: safe iteration [10]

Remove archived Book objects while traversing. Explain why direct removal in for-each is unsafe and show the Iterator version.

## Question 5: choose an implementation [12]

For four scenarios, choose ArrayList, ArrayDeque, HashSet/HashMap, or TreeSet/TreeMap. Justify the dominant operation, ordering requirement, and trade-off.
