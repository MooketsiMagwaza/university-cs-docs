## Before you start

You should be able to:

- explain why exact clock time is not a machine-independent comparison;
- distinguish experimentation from methodological analysis;
- identify primitive operations and the input size N;
- distinguish best, average, and worst cases;
- analyze sequential statements, selections, single loops, and nested loops;
- keep the dominant term and remove constants and lower-order terms;
- rank the common Big-O growth rates.
