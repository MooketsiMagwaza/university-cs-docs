## Question 1: stack state [10]

Trace <code>push(A), push(B), peek(), pop(), push(C), pop()</code>. Draw the stack and label top after every operation.

## Question 2: balanced brackets [12]

Use a stack to trace <code>&#123;[(a+b) * c]&#125;</code> and <code>&#123;[(])&#125;</code>. Show the stack after every bracket and identify the exact mismatch.

## Question 3: array stack [14]

Implement push, pop, and peek for capacity 4 with <code>top == -1</code> representing empty. Show overflow and underflow behaviour and clear removed reference cells.

## Question 4: circular queue [16]

For capacity 5, trace six offers and three polls in an order that causes rear to wrap. Show front, rear, size, physical array, and logical queue after every operation.

## Question 5: choose one [8]

Choose stack or queue for undo, printer jobs, breadth-first traversal, and nested-bracket checking. Justify each using the removal rule.
