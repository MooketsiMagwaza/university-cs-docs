## Chapter 3: Functions and Input/Output

Work through the lessons in order. Each lesson assumes the preceding lesson's vocabulary.

| Order | Lesson | Main question |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | [Defining Functions](/docs/sem3/csi243/03-defining-functions/notes/defining-functions) | How do signatures, parameters, and expressions define a reusable transformation? |
| 2 | [Terminal Output](/docs/sem3/csi243/03-defining-functions/notes/output) | What is the difference between `putStr`, `putStrLn`, `print`, and `show`? |
| 3 | [A Complete Program](/docs/sem3/csi243/03-defining-functions/notes/complete-program) | What do `main`, `IO ()`, `do`, comments, and indentation mean? |
| 4 | [Input, Bindings, and Processing](/docs/sem3/csi243/03-defining-functions/notes/input-processing) | How does data move through input, pure processing, and output? |

Complete [Lab 01: Input, Process, Output](/docs/sem3/csi243/03-defining-functions/notes/lab-01), then use [Practice Questions](/docs/sem3/csi243/03-defining-functions/questions) and [Review](/docs/sem3/csi243/03-defining-functions/review).

## Chapter outcomes

- Define and test a typed pure function.
- Select the appropriate output function.
- Explain every component of a complete `main` action.
- Use `getLine`, `<-`, `let`, `read`, `show`, and `++` in context.
- Trace an input–process–output pipeline.
