---
title: "Group Research and Presentation"
description: "Organising a group project: roles and workflow, meetings, integrating the final product, presenting together and peer accountability."
---

A group project needs shared ownership of the outcome and explicit responsibility for the work. This topic covers how to agree the question and the standards, divide and track tasks, run useful meetings, combine contributions into one product and present it together. It also covers what to do when a contribution is late.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Agree the question, deliverables, standards, milestones, communication channel and way of resolving disagreement before the work fragments.",
  "Set up roles and a task board that gives each task an owner, a definition of done, a dependency and a due date.",
  "Run a meeting with an agenda, and record decisions, action owners and deadlines.",
  "Integrate individual contributions into one product with a shared thesis, terminology, visual style and citation method.",
  "Plan a joint presentation with planned handovers, rehearsed timing and one purpose per slide.",
  "Give feedback on observable work and its impact, and escalate persistent non-participation with evidence of attempts to resolve it.",
]} />

<Prerequisites items={[
  "Topic 4: Argument and Evidence (a shared thesis supported by evidence)",
  "Topic 5: Research and Referencing (a source table and one citation method)",
]} />
