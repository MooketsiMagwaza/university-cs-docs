---
title: "Paragraphs and Essays"
description: "How a paragraph develops one idea, how an essay is organised from introduction to conclusion, and how to keep the whole coherent."
---

A paragraph is a unit of reasoning, not just a block of sentences: it needs a controlling idea and enough development for the reader to see why the idea matters. This topic shows how to build a paragraph, how paragraphs combine into an essay with an introduction, a body and a conclusion, and how to keep the whole piece coherent.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Write a paragraph with a topic sentence that makes a claim, followed by evidence, explanation and a link to the thesis.",
  "Use transitions to show addition, contrast, cause, qualification or consequence, rather than as decoration.",
  "Structure an essay with an introduction that ends in a thesis or roadmap, body sections that each advance part of the argument, and a conclusion that answers the question.",
  "Improve coherence through consistent key terms, clear pronoun reference and logical order, and organise a comparison point by point or subject by subject.",
  "Apply the paragraph test to find sentences that should be moved, rewritten or removed.",
]} />
