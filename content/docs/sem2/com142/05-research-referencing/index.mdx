---
title: "Research and Referencing"
description: "Searching for and evaluating sources, synthesising them around an idea, and citing and referencing them accurately and ethically."
---

Research is a traceable process: define a question, search strategically, evaluate sources, extract evidence, synthesise it and document where it came from. This topic covers each step, together with the citation and referencing practice that makes your use of sources checkable. Your argument is only as credible as the sources behind it and the way you acknowledge them.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Break a research question into concepts and synonyms, and search the library catalogue and databases for scholarly sources.",
  "Evaluate a source for authority, method, currency, relevance and purpose instead of relying on appearance or search ranking.",
  "Synthesise sources by grouping them around a claim, an agreement, a disagreement, a method or a limitation, using a matrix.",
  "Write in-text citations and a reference list in the required style.",
  "Use sources ethically: paraphrase with attribution, quote exactly, cite data, images and code, and never invent a reference.",
  "Audit a draft by comparing every in-text citation with the reference list and checking that the evidence supports the sentence.",
]} />
