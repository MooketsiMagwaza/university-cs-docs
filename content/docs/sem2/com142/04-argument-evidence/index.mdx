---
title: "Argument and Evidence"
description: "Building an argument from a specific thesis, reasons and evidence, answering counterarguments, and integrating sources into your own paragraphs."
---

An argument is a claim supported by reasons and evidence. A thesis has to be specific enough to guide an essay and contestable enough to need support. This topic covers the chain from claim to evidence to warrant, how to judge evidence, how to treat counterarguments fairly, how to spot weak reasoning, and how to bring sources into your paragraphs.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Distinguish a topic from a thesis, and write a thesis that is specific and contestable.",
  "Build a chain for each major point: the claim, the evidence, the warrant that links them, and a limitation or alternative.",
  "Judge evidence as relevant, sufficient, credible and current for the claim it supports.",
  "Represent an opposing view accurately, and respond by qualification or rebuttal.",
  "Recognise common fallacies, such as hasty generalisation, false cause, false dilemma and circular reasoning, and explain the specific weakness instead of only naming it.",
  "Integrate a source by introducing it, quoting or paraphrasing only the relevant part, citing it and explaining its role.",
]} />
