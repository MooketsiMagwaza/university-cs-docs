title: "Applications of Integration"
description: "Area between curves, volumes by discs, washers and shells, average value, and displacement versus total distance."
---

The definite integral accumulates a signed rate or area, so it can be used to measure regions, solids and totals. This topic uses integrals to find the area between curves, the volume of a solid of revolution by discs, washers and shells, the average value of a function, and displacement and distance from velocity. Sketching the situation first prevents most errors with limits and radii.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Set up and evaluate the area between two curves with vertical or horizontal slices, finding the intersections and splitting the integral where the curves cross.",
  "Find the volume of a solid of revolution by the disk and washer methods.",
  "Use the shell method for a region revolved around a vertical axis.",
  "Calculate the average value of a function on an interval.",
  "Distinguish displacement from total distance, splitting the integral where the velocity changes sign.",
  "Check a result using units (square for area, cubic for volume) and the scale and sign of a sketch.",
]} />
