---
title: "Applications of Differentiation"
description: "Critical numbers, increasing and decreasing intervals, concavity and extrema, optimisation problems and linear approximation."
---

The sign of the first derivative tells where a function rises and falls, and the sign of the second derivative tells how its graph bends. This topic uses those signs to classify critical points, find absolute extrema on closed intervals, solve optimisation problems and approximate a function near a point. It turns the rules of differentiation into answers about the shape of a graph and the best choice in a problem.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Find the critical numbers of a function, and explain why they are only candidates for local extrema.",
  "Use a sign chart for the first derivative to find where a function is increasing or decreasing and to classify local maxima and minima.",
  "Use the second derivative to find concavity, and explain why an inflection point needs a change in concavity.",
  "Find the absolute extrema of a continuous function on a closed interval by comparing its values at the critical numbers and the endpoints.",
  "Solve an optimisation problem by drawing the situation, defining the quantity, writing it in one variable, stating the domain, classifying the critical points and reporting the answer with units.",
  "Use linear approximation near a point, and explain how the error grows away from it.",
]} />
