---
title: "Coordinate Geometry"
description: "Slopes and equations of lines, parallel and perpendicular lines, distance and midpoint, circles and conics, and intersections of curves."
---

Coordinate geometry describes lines, circles and other curves by equations in the plane. This topic covers the slope and standard forms of a line, parallel and perpendicular lines, the distance and midpoint formulas, the equation of a circle and the recognition of a conic, and finding where two curves meet. It applies algebra to geometric questions and checks the algebra against the original problem.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Find the slope of a line through two points and write its equation in point-slope and slope-intercept forms, including vertical and horizontal lines.",
  "Decide whether two lines are parallel or perpendicular by comparing slopes, taking care with vertical and horizontal lines.",
  "Calculate the distance between two points and the midpoint of the segment joining them.",
  "Write the equation of a circle, and complete the square to recognise one from a quadratic equation.",
  "Interpret the equation of a parabola, an ellipse or a hyperbola by inspecting its squared terms and signs.",
  "Find where two curves meet by solving their equations simultaneously, and check each candidate in both original equations.",
]} />
