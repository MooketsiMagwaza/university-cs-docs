title: "Integration"
description: "Antiderivatives and the indefinite integral, the definite integral as signed area, the fundamental theorem, substitution and integration by parts."
---

An antiderivative of a function is a function whose derivative it is, and the definite integral is a limit of signed areas. This topic covers the basic rules of integration, the fundamental theorem that connects the two ideas, and the methods of substitution and integration by parts, which reverse the chain rule and the product rule. Wherever possible an integral is checked by differentiating the answer.
