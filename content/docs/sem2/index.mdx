Semester II extends the foundations from Semester I into formal reasoning, object-oriented design, calculus, probability, and academic writing.

## Courses

- **CSI132: Discrete Structures II** — proof methods, sequences, counting, relations, graphs, and number theory.
- **CSI142: Object-Oriented Programming** — Java methods, classes, collections, exceptions, and reusable software design.
- **MAT122: Introductory Mathematics II** — limits, differentiation, integration, applications, and coordinate geometry.
- **STA122: Introductory Concepts of Probability** — probability models, random variables, and discrete and continuous distributions.
- **COM142: Academic Writing** — planning, structuring, researching, drafting, revising, and referencing academic texts.

> **Navigation:** Course outlines, lecture packs, tutorials, assignments, and study notes are grouped inside each course.
