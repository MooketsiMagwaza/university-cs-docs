---
title: "Sets and Functions"
description: "Set operations and De Morgan's laws, Cartesian products and relations, and injective, surjective and bijective functions with composition and inverses."
---

A set is a collection of distinct objects, and sets, relations and functions are the language in which the later topics are stated. This topic revisits set operations, ordered pairs and relations, and functions with their domain, codomain and range, and shows how to prove that a function is injective, surjective or bijective. It builds on the formal reasoning introduced in CSI131.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Use set notation, subset, union, intersection, difference and complement (relative to a stated universal set), and apply De Morgan's laws.",
  "Form Cartesian products while keeping the order of each pair, and describe a relation as a subset of a product.",
  "Test a relation for reflexivity, symmetry and transitivity.",
  "Distinguish the domain, codomain and range of a function, and classify it as injective, surjective or bijective.",
  "Prove from the definitions that a function such as a linear one is injective and surjective, and find its inverse.",
  "Compose functions, and say when an inverse exists for the stated domain and codomain.",
]} />
