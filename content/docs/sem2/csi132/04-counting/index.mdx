title: "Counting"
description: "The product and sum rules, permutations and combinations, repetition, complementary counting and the count for two overlapping sets."
---

Counting arguments find how many outcomes are possible without listing them all. The skill lies in choosing the right model first: whether the choices are sequential or alternative, whether order matters, and whether repetition is allowed. This topic gives the rules for each case, and a set of questions that stop you applying a memorised formula to the wrong model.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Apply the product rule and the sum rule, checking that alternatives do not overlap before adding.",
  "Choose between permutations and combinations by deciding whether order matters, and compute both.",
  "Count ordered strings when repetition is allowed.",
  "Use complementary counting when a restriction is easier to describe by its opposite.",
  "Count the union of two overlapping sets by subtracting the intersection that was counted twice, using a Venn diagram.",
  "Work through the decision questions (what is one outcome, does order matter, is repetition allowed, do cases overlap) and check an answer on a smaller instance.",
]} />
