title: "Number Theory"
description: "Divisibility, the division algorithm and the Euclidean algorithm, congruences and modular arithmetic, and prime factorisation."
---

Number theory studies the integers through divisibility. This topic covers the division algorithm and the Euclidean algorithm for greatest common divisors, congruences and the rules for calculating modulo $m$, and unique prime factorisation. Throughout, care is needed over what remainders, cancellation and primes really allow.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "State and use the definition of divisibility, including that it is transitive.",
  "Apply the division algorithm to find a unique quotient and a remainder that is non-negative and smaller than the divisor.",
  "Compute a greatest common divisor with the Euclidean algorithm, and use back-substitution to write it as an integer combination.",
  "Work with congruences by adding, multiplying and reducing before calculating, and say when cancelling a factor is valid.",
  "Use prime factorisation to find divisors, greatest common divisors (minimum exponents) and least common multiples (maximum exponents).",
]} />
