title: "Sequences and Summations"
description: "Sequence notation, arithmetic and geometric sums, sigma notation, recurrence relations and recursive algorithms."
---

A sequence is a function from a set of integers into a target set, and in a sequence order and repetition matter. This topic covers arithmetic and geometric sequences and their sums, the careful manipulation of sigma notation, recurrence relations with their initial conditions, and recursion with a base case and a step that moves toward it. Showing that a formula or pattern really holds relies on induction from the previous topic.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Use sequence notation correctly, taking care with zero-based and one-based indices.",
  "Write the general term and the finite sum of an arithmetic or a geometric sequence, handling the case where the ratio is 1.",
  "Split sums, pull out constants and shift indices in sigma notation without losing a boundary term.",
  "Solve a recurrence by repeated expansion, stating its initial conditions.",
  "Verify a closed form for a recurrence by induction instead of assuming that a pattern continues.",
  "Trace a recursive algorithm such as factorial through both the calls and the returns.",
]} />
