---
title: "Continuous Probability Distributions"
description: "Probability density functions, probabilities as areas, the uniform and normal distributions, standardising, and the central limit theorem."
---

A continuous random variable is described by a probability density function, and probabilities are areas under it rather than values of it. This topic covers densities and the cumulative distribution function, the uniform and normal distributions, standardising a normal variable, and the central limit theorem, which explains why the normal curve appears so often. The difference between density and probability is the main new idea.
