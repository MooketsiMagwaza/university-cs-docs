---
title: "Conditional Probability and Independence"
description: "Conditional probability and the multiplication rule, independence, the law of total probability, and Bayes' theorem."
---

Conditional probability updates the chance of an event after learning that another has occurred. This topic covers the definition and the multiplication rule, independence, the law of total probability and Bayes' theorem, with tree diagrams to organise the calculations. It shows why a positive result from a fairly accurate test can still leave a rare condition unlikely, because the base rate matters as well as the accuracy of the test.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Calculate a conditional probability from its definition, and use the multiplication rule along the branches of a tree diagram.",
  "Test two events for independence, and explain why disjoint events of positive probability are not independent.",
  "Apply the law of total probability to a partition of the sample space.",
  "Use Bayes' theorem to reverse a conditional probability, including the calculation of the denominator.",
  "Interpret a test result using the base rate of the condition.",
  "Avoid the common errors of reversing the two conditional probabilities and failing to condition the second draw when sampling without replacement.",
]} />
