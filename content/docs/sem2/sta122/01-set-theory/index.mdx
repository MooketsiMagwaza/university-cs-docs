---
title: "Set Theory and Events"
description: "Sample spaces and events described with sets, De Morgan's laws, and Venn and tree diagrams for organising outcomes."
---

Probability begins with a sample space $\Omega$, the set of possible outcomes, and an event is a subset of it. This topic uses set language to describe events, shows how to define outcomes precisely, and introduces Venn and tree diagrams for organising overlapping and sequential situations. Every later calculation depends on stating the sample space clearly.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Define a sample space precisely for an experiment, and distinguish an outcome from an event.",
  "Express events with complement, union and intersection, and recognise disjoint events.",
  "Use De Morgan's laws to translate complements of unions and intersections.",
  "Represent overlapping events with a Venn diagram and sequential experiments with a tree diagram.",
  "Check that the terminal branches of a tree diagram are mutually exclusive and exhaustive.",
  "Explain why one situation can have different valid sample spaces, and keep probabilities consistent within the chosen model.",
]} />
