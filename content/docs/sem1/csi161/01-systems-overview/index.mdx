---
title: "Computing Systems and the Information Cycle"
description: "How a computer system combines hardware, software, data, people and procedures, and how input, processing, output and storage form the information cycle."
---

A computer system combines hardware, software, data, people and procedures to turn input into useful output. This topic introduces the information cycle of input, processing, output and storage, with communication linking the stages, and the habit of analysing a whole system rather than a single device. It is the frame in which the architecture, software and networking topics fit.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Define a computer system, and name the four stages of the information cycle and the role of communication.",
  "Distinguish hardware from software, and explain why each depends on the other.",
  "Distinguish data from information, using an example such as a number that has no meaning until its context is known.",
  "Apply the information cycle to an everyday situation such as submitting an online form.",
  "Decompose a technology into its user, input, processing steps, output, storage, communication links and failure points.",
  "Explain how changing one component of a system affects the rest.",
]} />
