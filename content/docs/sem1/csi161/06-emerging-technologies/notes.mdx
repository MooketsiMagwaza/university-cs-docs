## Emerging Technologies

Emerging technologies combine familiar computing foundations in new ways. Evaluate them by asking what problem they solve, what data and infrastructure they require, and what risks they introduce.

## Cloud computing

Cloud services provide computing resources over a network. Infrastructure as a service offers virtual machines and storage; platform as a service provides development environments; software as a service delivers applications. Benefits include elasticity and access, while risks include outages, vendor dependence, privacy, and cost drift.

## Artificial intelligence

An AI system uses data, rules, or learned models to produce predictions or decisions. A training set, objective, evaluation method, and deployment context all influence quality. A model can be accurate on familiar data yet fail on new or biased data.

## Connected and ubiquitous computing

Sensors, mobile devices, and embedded systems collect data continuously. The design must consider energy, connectivity, privacy, safety, and the consequences of automation.

## Responsible evaluation

Ask who benefits, who bears the risk, what assumptions are hidden, and how a person can contest an automated decision. Technical possibility is not the same as social desirability.

## Checkpoint

For a proposed technology, write a short assessment containing the architecture, data flow, expected benefit, failure mode, privacy concern, and mitigation.
