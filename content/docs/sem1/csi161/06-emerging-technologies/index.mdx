---
title: "Emerging Technologies"
description: "Cloud computing, artificial intelligence and connected computing, and how to evaluate a new technology for benefit, risk and responsible use."
---

Emerging technologies combine familiar computing foundations in new ways. This topic looks at cloud computing, artificial intelligence and connected, ubiquitous computing, and gives a way to evaluate any of them by the problem it solves, the data and infrastructure it needs and the risks it introduces. Technical possibility is not the same as social desirability, so the topic closes with questions of responsibility.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Describe cloud computing, and distinguish infrastructure, platform and software as a service.",
  "Weigh the benefits of cloud services, such as elasticity and access, against their risks: outages, vendor dependence, privacy and cost drift.",
  "Explain how an AI system produces predictions or decisions, and why the training set, objective, evaluation method and deployment context affect its quality.",
  "Explain why a model that is accurate on familiar data can fail on new or biased data.",
  "Identify the design concerns of sensors, mobile devices and embedded systems: energy, connectivity, privacy, safety and automation.",
  "Write a short assessment of a proposed technology covering its architecture, data flow, benefit, failure mode, privacy concern and mitigation.",
]} />
