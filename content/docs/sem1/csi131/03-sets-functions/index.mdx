title: "Sets, Functions, Relations, and Graphs"
description: "Sets and set operations, Cartesian products and relations, injective, surjective and bijective functions, composition and inverses, and graph models."
---

A set is a collection of distinct objects, and sets, relations, functions and graphs are the basic structures used to model data in computer science. This topic covers the notation and operations for each, and how to prove statements about them, for example by double inclusion. It also shows how relations and graphs are turned into data structures such as adjacency matrices and lists.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Use set notation and carry out union, intersection, complement, difference and power-set operations.",
  "Prove that two sets are equal by double inclusion.",
  "Form Cartesian products and relations, and test whether a relation is reflexive, symmetric, antisymmetric or transitive.",
  "Decide whether a rule is a function, and classify a function as injective, surjective or bijective, naming its codomain.",
  "Compose functions in the correct order, and find and check the inverse of a bijection.",
  "Describe a graph using vertices, edges, degrees, paths and cycles, and say how adjacency matrices and lists represent it.",
]} />
