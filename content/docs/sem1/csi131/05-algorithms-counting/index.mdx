---
title: "Algorithms and Counting"
description: "Designing and specifying algorithms, searching and sorting, growth rates, and the basic rules for counting outcomes."
---

An algorithm is a finite, unambiguous sequence of effective steps from input to output, and a good specification says what it assumes, what it delivers and why it stops. This topic covers designing and checking algorithms, the standard search and sort methods and how their running times compare, and the principles for counting outcomes. Together they let you reason both about what a procedure does and about how much work it takes.
