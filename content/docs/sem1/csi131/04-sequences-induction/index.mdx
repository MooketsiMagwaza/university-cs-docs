title: "Sequences, Summations, and Recursion"
description: "Arithmetic and geometric sequences, recurrence relations, sigma notation, and recursive definitions and algorithms."
---

A sequence is a function whose domain is usually the positive or non-negative integers, and many discrete structures are described by defining their terms one after another. This topic covers common sequences and their sums, recurrence relations, sigma notation and recursion. Recursion is the link between these formulas and the induction proofs met in the topic on proof techniques.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Write the general term of an arithmetic or a geometric sequence and find its sum.",
  "Generate terms from a recurrence relation and its initial conditions, and check a proposed closed form against both.",
  "Use sigma notation and its laws, including the standard sums of the first n integers and of their squares, and adjust the bounds when an index is shifted.",
  "Define a sequence or function recursively with a base case and a recursive case that makes progress toward it.",
  "Trace a recursive computation such as factorial by expanding the calls down to the base case.",
  "Prove a formula for a sequence by induction, from the base case through the assumption at k to the step at k plus 1.",
]} />
