---
title: "Logic and Inference"
description: "Propositions, connectives, truth tables, logical equivalence, predicates and quantifiers, and valid rules of inference."
---

Logic gives us a precise language for conditions. A proposition is a statement that is either true or false, and this topic builds from propositions and connectives to truth tables, equivalence, quantified statements and valid arguments. The same reasoning supports the proofs in the rest of the course and the checking of conditions in a system.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Translate a statement into propositional form using negation, conjunction, disjunction, exclusive or, implication and the biconditional.",
  "Build a truth table with a column for each intermediate expression, and use it to test equivalence, tautologies and contradictions.",
  "Apply De Morgan's laws, and negate an implication as the hypothesis together with the negated conclusion.",
  "Express a statement with predicates and quantifiers, state its domain, and negate it by changing both the quantifier and the predicate.",
  "Decide whether an argument is valid using modus ponens, modus tollens, hypothetical syllogism and disjunctive syllogism.",
  "Spot common errors such as affirming the consequent, reading 'or' as exclusive, and leaving out the domain.",
]} />
