The unit circle defines sine and cosine for every real angle, which is what makes the trigonometric functions periodic. This topic covers the core identities, the sine and cosine rules for triangles, solving equations within an interval and finding the other solutions by periodicity, and reading the amplitude, midline and range of a sine curve. Care with radians, intervals and quadrants runs through all of it.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Use the unit circle to define sine and cosine, and state the periods of sine, cosine and tangent.",
  "Apply the Pythagorean identities and the sum, difference and double-angle formulas.",
  "Use the sine rule and the cosine rule to solve a triangle, matching each side to its opposite angle and watching for the ambiguous case.",
  "Solve a trigonometric equation in a stated interval, and use periodicity to find the other solutions.",
  "Read the amplitude, period, shift, midline and range from a sine or cosine graph.",
]} />
