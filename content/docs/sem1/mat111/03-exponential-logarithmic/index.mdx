---
title: "Exponential and Logarithmic Functions"
description: "Exponential and logarithmic functions, their laws and graphs, and the methods for solving exponential and logarithmic equations."
---

The exponential function $a^x$ and the logarithm $\log_a x$ are inverses of each other, and together they describe growth and let us solve for an unknown exponent. This topic covers the laws of exponents and logarithms, the domain conditions they depend on, the graphs and asymptotes of each, and the methods for solving equations. A common thread is checking every answer against the domain of the original equation.
