---
title: "Polynomial and Rational Functions"
description: "Polynomials and their end behaviour, the factor and remainder theorems, and rational functions with their holes and asymptotes."
---

A polynomial is built from non-negative integer powers of $x$, and a rational function is a quotient of two polynomials. This topic uses the remainder and factor theorems to test candidate roots and reduce a polynomial by division, and then studies rational functions: where they are undefined, where holes and vertical asymptotes occur, and how horizontal asymptotes follow from the degrees. It shows how algebra predicts the shape of a graph.

  "Describe a polynomial by its degree and leading coefficient, and relate them to its end behaviour.",

  "Find a horizontal asymptote by comparing the degrees and leading coefficients of numerator and denominator.",
  "Sketch a rational function from its zeros, holes and asymptotes, remembering that a cancelled factor still leaves its input excluded.",
]} />
