title: "Functions and Graphs"
description: "Functions, domain and range, one-to-one and onto functions, composition and inverses, and graph transformations."
---

A function assigns exactly one output to each input, and the rest of the course is largely a study of particular families of functions. This topic covers finding domains and ranges, deciding whether a function is one-to-one or onto, composing functions and finding inverses, and reading how translations and reflections change a graph. It is the foundation for the polynomial, exponential, trigonometric and complex-number topics that follow.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Find the domain of a function by excluding zero denominators, negative numbers under even roots and non-positive logarithm arguments, and find its range algebraically or graphically.",
  "Use the horizontal-line test to decide whether a function is one-to-one, and explain why being onto depends on the stated codomain.",
  "Compose two functions in the correct order and state the domain of the result.",
  "Find an inverse by solving for x, and verify it by composing in both directions on the correct domains.",
  "Describe and sketch vertical and horizontal shifts and reflections of a graph, working from the base graph.",
  "Sketch a reciprocal function such as 1/(x - 2) with its excluded value and its asymptotes.",
]} />
