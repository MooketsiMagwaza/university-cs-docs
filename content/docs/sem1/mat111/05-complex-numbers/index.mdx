title: "Complex Numbers"

---

A complex number has the form $a+bi$ with $i^2=-1$, and it can be plotted as a point in the plane as well as handled algebraically. This topic covers arithmetic and conjugates, the modulus and argument that give polar form, De Moivre's theorem for powers, and the $n$ equally spaced roots of a complex number. It joins algebra to geometry and uses the trigonometry of the previous topic.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Add, subtract, multiply and divide complex numbers, using the conjugate to divide.",
  "Plot a complex number on an Argand diagram, and find its modulus and its argument in the correct quadrant.",
  "Write a complex number in polar form using its modulus and argument.",
  "Use De Moivre's theorem to raise a complex number to a power.",
  "Find all n roots of a complex number and place them on a circle in the Argand plane.",
]} />
