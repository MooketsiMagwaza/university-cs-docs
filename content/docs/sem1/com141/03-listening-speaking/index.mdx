---
title: "Listening, Note-Taking, and Speaking"
description: "Active listening, note-making methods for lectures, and how to plan and deliver a short presentation and handle questions."
---

Listening is an active process of attending, interpreting and responding, and a presentation is a planned act of speaking for a particular audience. This topic covers how to listen in a lecture and make selective notes, and how to plan, build and deliver a talk. These skills support learning in lectures and the structured oral presentations that the course asks for.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Preview a lecture topic and listen for definitions, examples, contrasts, reasons and transitions.",
  "Make notes with the Cornell, outline or concept-map method, and say which suits a given kind of material.",
  "Plan a presentation around its audience and purpose, with an opening, two to four main points and a close.",
  "Design slides that support the speech: readable text, meaningful diagrams, consistent labels and cited media.",
  "Deliver a talk with a controlled pace and pauses, and answer a question honestly when you are not certain of the answer.",
]} />
