---
title: "Communication in Academic Contexts"
description: "The communication process, the verbal, non-verbal and written modes, common barriers, and how to repair a misunderstanding."
---

Communication is the purposeful exchange of meaning, and it is not complete until the receiver has interpreted the message. This topic breaks the process into sender, message, channel, receiver, feedback and context, and shows where noise and barriers enter. It matters because group work, tutorials and assessments all depend on a message being understood the way it was meant.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Identify the elements of the communication process (sender, message, encoding, channel, receiver, decoding, feedback and context) in a concrete situation.",
  "Compare verbal, non-verbal and written communication, and choose a channel that suits the task.",
  "Recognise common barriers such as ambiguous language, jargon, cultural assumptions and poor timing.",
  "Repair a misunderstanding by restating the message, asking an open question and checking the interpretation.",
  "Separate observation from interpretation and interpretation from conclusion, and support a claim with a source or a labelled example.",
]} />
