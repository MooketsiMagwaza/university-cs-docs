title: "University Learning and Study Practice"
description: "How to learn at university: the learning cycle, setting goals, managing time, asking for help and keeping to academic integrity."
---

University study is more self-directed than school study. Lectures introduce ideas, but the learning is completed through preparation, practice, feedback and reflection. This topic sets out the habits that make that work effective: a learning cycle, specific goals, realistic time management, and early use of lecturers, tutors and support services.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Describe the learning cycle of prepare, engage, practise, check and reflect, and name the evidence of progress at each stage.",
  "Use active recall instead of rereading to test what you can really reproduce from memory.",
  "Write a goal that is specific, observable and time-bounded, and break it into weekly outcomes and study blocks.",
  "Ask a lecturer or tutor a question that includes your own attempt, so that they can diagnose the misconception.",
  "Keep an error log and a weekly review so that mistakes are recorded and acted on rather than kept private.",
  "Mark the source of every idea in your notes so that borrowed wording is never mistaken for your own.",
]} />
