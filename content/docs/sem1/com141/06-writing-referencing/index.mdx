---
title: "Academic Writing and Ethical Referencing"
description: "Turning an assignment into an argument, building paragraphs, paraphrasing and summarising, revising, and avoiding plagiarism through referencing."
---

Academic writing makes a claim, supports it with evidence, explains the connection and acknowledges every source. It is a chain of reasoning rather than a collection of quotations. This topic covers unpacking an assignment, building a paragraph, paraphrasing and summarising, revising in passes and referencing in one consistent style.

<LearningObjectives items={[
  "Unpack an assignment verb (describe, compare, analyse or evaluate) and turn the prompt into a working question with a provisional answer.",
  "Write a paragraph with a topic sentence, evidence, explanation and a link to the thesis or the next idea.",
  "Paraphrase and summarise a source accurately, and quote directly with quotation marks and a location.",
  "Revise in passes: argument and structure first, then paragraph reasoning, sentence clarity and proofreading.",
  "Identify plagiarism and its common causes, and prevent it by separating source material from your own commentary.",
  "Match every in-text citation to a reference entry in the style the course requires.",
]} />

  "Topic 5: Information Literacy and Source Evaluation (evaluating sources and keeping a research record)",
]} />
